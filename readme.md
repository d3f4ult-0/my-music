MyMusic

MyMusic is a local-first, cross-platform music player built around your own music library.

It is designed to give you complete control over your music, playback, data, and listening history without requiring accounts, subscriptions, ads, or cloud storage.

Features

- 🎵 FLAC-first playback with support for common audio formats.
- 📚 Automatic library scanning and metadata extraction.
- 🎨 Album artwork and detailed track information.
- 🎧 Full playback controls, including queues, shuffle, repeat, and resume.
- ❤️ Favorites and custom playlists.
- 📝 Lyrics integration with local caching.
- 📊 Detailed listening statistics and listening history.
- 📅 Monthly and yearly listening reports.
- 💾 Offline downloads and playback.
- 🖥️ Cross-platform desktop support for Linux and Windows.
- 📱 Android client.
- 🌐 Optional LAN library sharing between devices.
- 🔒 No accounts, subscriptions, ads, or centralized cloud library.

Philosophy

Your music. Your files. Your data. Your statistics.

MyMusic treats the user's music collection as the source of truth rather than trying to replace it with a centralized streaming catalog.

The desktop application can host the local library and API, while other devices can connect directly over the local network.

This is a personal project, built primarily for experimentation, learning, and creating a music ecosystem that I actually want to use.
